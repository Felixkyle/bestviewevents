ABOUT THE COMPANY

Kadis Events, Media & Rentals

Kadis Events, Media & Rentals is the production and events arm of Kadis — the team that plans, shoots, edits, stages and streams for weddings, corporates, schools and concerts across Abuja, and equips anyone else's crew through equipment rentals. Every booking is run by our own in-house team, on our own tested gear, from the first phone call to the final delivered file.

Our Team

Photographers, videographers, editors and stage crew, all in-house — not a different freelancer for every booking.

Our Equipment

Cameras, lighting, audio and staging owned and tested by us, so what you rent is exactly what shoots your job.

Why Choose Us

One quote, one crew, one team accountable for the final delivery — from the first call to the finished file.

Our Clients

Corporates, schools, wedding couples and event organisers across Abuja who book us again for the next one.
